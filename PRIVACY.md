# Privacy Policy — Plume RSS Reader

**Last updated: May 2026**

## Summary

Plume does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. All data is stored locally on your device.

## Data Storage

Plume stores the following data locally in your browser using IndexedDB:

- RSS/Atom feed URLs you subscribe to
- Article content downloaded from those feeds
- Your read/saved article status
- Extension settings (theme, sync preferences)

This data never leaves your device and is never sent to any server controlled by the developer.

## Network Requests

Plume makes network requests solely to:

- Fetch RSS/Atom feed content from the feed URLs you have subscribed to
- Fetch full article content from source websites when you request it
- Query the Feedly public search API (`cloud.feedly.com`) when you search for feeds by name — no account or personal data is required or sent

No analytics, tracking pixels, or telemetry of any kind are included in this extension.

## Third-Party Services

Plume does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or data collection services.

## Data Deletion

You can delete all locally stored data at any time via **Settings → Clear all cached articles**. Uninstalling the extension removes all associated data from your browser.
